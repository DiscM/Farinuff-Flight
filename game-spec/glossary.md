# Portable Glossary

Status: canonical vocabulary

**Combat Space** — The bounded area in which the Player Craft and Enemy Craft move and exchange attacks.

**Combat Plane** — The horizontal gameplay plane used by a top-down 3D presentation. Vertical height is visual unless the design explicitly adds it to gameplay.

**Design unit** — A portable tuning unit mapped by each implementation to pixels, world units, tiles, or another coordinate system.

**Enemy Craft** — A hostile ship that participates in an encounter and can attack or be destroyed.

**Endless** — Optional play after the authored Wave-20 Expedition climax.

**Expedition** — The finite authored campaign whose initial target is defeating the Wave-20 Tempest Core.

**Flight School** — The replayable onboarding flow that teaches controls, reflection, orb/life economy, and build decisions.

**Interaction Range** — The distance within which an incoming Projectile can affect or be actively responded to by the Player Craft.

**Player Craft** — The ship controlled by the player during a run.

**Power-Up** — A temporary or one-shot combat benefit collected during a run.

**Projectile** — A traveling attack launched by a craft or encounter hazard. It may damage, be deflected, or trigger impact feedback.

**Run** — One session from launch through Expedition completion, Endless exit, or final game over.

**Run Upgrade** — A run-long enhancement acquired during milestone decisions.

**Elite Upgrade** — A milestone Run Upgrade that changes the Player Craft's combat identity or capability.

**Salvage** — Persistent currency earned from boss kills and finalized run results.

**Ship Variant** — A persistent sidegrade selected before a run.

**Challenge Modifier** — A persistent unlock that increases run difficulty in exchange for a Salvage multiplier.

**Gameplay Hitbox** — The simplified contact envelope used by simulation. It does not need to match visible geometry.

**Visual Partner** — The visual or audio feedback associated with a simulation event.
